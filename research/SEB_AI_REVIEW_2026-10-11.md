## Improvements to Trading-for-You

These are proposed next changes, not implemented integrations or performance claims.

1. Evidence report: reconcile persisted balances/fills first, then compare synchronized equity against passive and cash benchmarks. Display mark age, costs, invalid days, actual natural round trips and experiment fingerprint. Keep operational health distinct from performance evidence.
2. News provenance: report source URL, publication time, receipt time, symbol, delay and reason for relevance. The current feed deliberately delays news by 15 minutes plus polling; do not market it as instantaneous news execution. Separate summaries from tested signals.
3. Paper decision card: one readable account-specific record of rule, completed input bar, decision timestamp, acceptable quote, position size, exposure and rejection reason. A technical label such as UP alone is insufficient to communicate the full decision.
4. Independent implementation checks: compare indicator/decision outputs with a second implementation; check future-input isolation and sufficient warmup. Do not tune parameters merely to improve the same inspected historical result.
5. Trader-copy evaluation: require independently attributable complete histories, positions, cash flows, timestamps and costs; model the follower's actual observation delay. A badge, selected screenshots or visible winning trades cannot qualify a trader. None is accepted yet.
